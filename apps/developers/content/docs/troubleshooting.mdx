title: Troubleshooting
description: Diagnose common publication, Delivery, Preview, CLI, generation, and webhook integration problems.
---

{/* Gives actionable recovery paths without exposing internal implementation details. */}

## Content Is Missing from Delivery

Confirm the exact locale is published, the collection schema is published, the entry is active, and your credential can read that project and environment. Draft saves do not change Delivery.

## A Reference Blocks Publication

Every present reference requires a current target publication in the exact source locale. Publish the target locale or remove/correct the draft reference; the platform never falls back to another locale.

## A Cursor Is Invalid or Stale

Do not edit or reuse cursors across queries. Restart intentionally from page one after a collection-locale publication change and reject repeated cursors in pagination loops.

## Preview Works but Delivery Does Not

Preview can show unpublished or soft-invalid drafts. Resolve returned validation issues and publish the exact locale before expecting Delivery to change.

## CLI Login Cannot Persist

Unlock or configure the native operating-system credential store. The CLI intentionally has no plaintext refresh-token fallback.

## Schema Pull Reports Concurrent Authority

A schema publication changed the manifest during reconciliation. Wait for publication to finish and retry. The CLI refuses to generate a mixed lock.

## Generated Files Were Modified

Review local changes and restore the owned output before regenerating. Ordinary generation will not silently overwrite unexpected edits or an unowned directory.

## Webhook Signatures Fail

Verify against exact raw request bytes, preserve all Standard Webhooks headers, use the correct active or retiring secret, and check clock skew. Do not parse and reserialize the body first.

## Need Support Correlation

Retain the response `requestId` and safe operation context. Never send credentials, authorization headers, signing secrets, or complete private content in support logs.
